DentaBiome Alternatives & Complements

We compare categories, not named competitor brands, so you can judge what fits your needs.

Fluoride toothpaste & flossing

The foundation of oral care, backed by public health agencies. This isn't really an alternative, since DentaBiome is meant to add to it.

Best for: Everyone

Sugar-free xylitol gum or mints

NIDCR notes that sugarless gums and candies with xylitol may help prevent cavities and can ease a dry mouth. They don't contain postbiotics.

Best for: People who mainly want a sugar-free, saliva-stimulating habit

Other oral probiotic lozenges

Other oral-focused probiotic products exist with different strains and doses. Compare labels, strain information and guarantees carefully.

Best for: Shoppers who want to compare strain lists

Mouthwash

Rinses can freshen breath short-term. Some contain alcohol, which may dry the mouth.

Best for: Quick freshness on the go

Professional dental cleanings

Removes hardened tartar that no supplement can. Essential for gum concerns.

Best for: Anyone with bleeding, receding or sore gums
