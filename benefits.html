Why consistency matters

Benefits Build With Daily Use

Oral care is cumulative. The bacteria in your mouth are constantly being replenished, so habits only count when they're repeated. That's why DentaBiome is built around a single daily chewable that's easy to keep up with.

The seller recommends at least 3 to 6 months of steady use for adults over 45 or anyone with existing concerns. The seller-supplied testimonials describe changes over days to weeks, but every mouth is different, and nothing is guaranteed.
