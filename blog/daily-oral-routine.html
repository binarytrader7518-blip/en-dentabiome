A Simple Daily Oral Care Routine That Sticks

Brushing, flossing, water and checkups, plus where an optional chewable can fit.

By the DentaBiome Info editorial team · Updated November 25, 2026 · 5 min read

In this article
  1. Simple beats perfect
  2. Morning
  3. During the day
  4. Evening
  5. Twice a year (or as advised)

Simple beats perfect

The best oral care routine is the one you'll actually follow. Public health guidance from the CDC and NIH points to a short list of habits that do most of the work. Here's how to make them stick.

Morning

  1. Brush for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  2. Brush or scrape your tongue.
  3. Take your daily supplement if you use one. A DentaBiome chewable fits well right after brushing.

During the day

  • Drink water, especially after coffee, tea or meals.
  • Choose sugar-free gum or mints over sugary ones.
  • Have sweets with meals rather than grazing on them all day.

Evening

  1. Floss or use interdental brushes.
  2. Brush for two minutes before bed.
  3. Skip eating and sugary drinks after your evening brush.

Twice a year (or as advised)

Professional cleanings remove hardened tartar that brushing can't, and checkups catch problems early. Bleeding, receding or sore gums are a reason to book a visit, not to wait it out.

Habit tip: keep your chewables next to your toothbrush. Linking a new habit to an existing one makes it far easier to remember.
