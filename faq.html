What is DentaBiome?

DentaBiome is a daily oral postbiotic supplement in a Berry Frost chewable tablet. It combines Lactobacillus-based postbiotic components, xylitol, purple carrot powder, cranberry extract and the BioFresh™ Clean Complex, and is meant to support a fresh, balanced mouth as part of a normal oral care routine. See the full product overview.

What ingredients are in DentaBiome?

The seller lists a Dual-Strain L. plantarum Complex, L. salivarius, L. rhamnosus, the BioFresh™ Clean Complex, xylitol, purple carrot powder and cranberry extract. The front label highlights purple carrot powder and cranberry extract and is marked gluten-free, soy-free and vegan. Individual amounts are not published.

How does DentaBiome work?

The chewable dissolves in your mouth, so its ingredients are in contact with your teeth and gums before you swallow. The Lactobacillus postbiotic blend is aimed at supporting a balanced oral microbiome, xylitol adds sugar-free sweetness, and the botanical extracts round out the formula. Read how it works.

What are the main benefits of DentaBiome?

The product is positioned to support fresh breath, comfortable gums and a balanced oral environment, in an easy chewable that fits into any morning or evening routine. Explore each one on our benefits page. Results vary, and the product does not replace dental care.

How do I take DentaBiome?

Take one Berry Frost chewable per day. Chew it thoroughly or let it dissolve slowly in your mouth. Do not double up on servings, and follow the directions on your bottle's label, which take priority over any website.

How long does one bottle last?

The bottle label shows 30 chewable tablets (net weight 1.66 oz / 48 g). At one tablet per day, one bottle is about a 30-day supply.

The seller suggests using DentaBiome consistently for at least 3 to 6 months, especially if you are over 45 or already dealing with oral concerns. Experiences vary from person to person, and no specific result or timeline is guaranteed.

Who is DentaBiome for?

It is designed for adults who want to add an easy, sugar-free step to their oral care routine, and especially for people who would rather chew a tablet than swallow capsules. Ask your dentist or doctor first if you are pregnant, nursing, take medication or have a diagnosed dental or medical condition.

Does DentaBiome have side effects?

The seller describes it as non-habit-forming, made in the USA in a GMP-certified facility, non-GMO, vegan and dairy-free. Xylitol can cause stomach upset in some people when taken in large amounts. Stop using it and talk to a professional if you notice any reaction.

Is DentaBiome safe around pets?

Keep it away from pets. DentaBiome contains xylitol, and the FDA warns that xylitol is dangerous for dogs. Store the bottle out of reach of children and animals.
