Understanding it

What Gum Comfort Involves

Gum tissue is constantly exposed to the bacteria that make up plaque. When plaque builds up along the gumline, the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research explains, it can lead to inflamed gums and, over time, gum disease. Good daily habits and professional cleanings are the foundation of gum care.

Source: Periodontal (Gum) Disease (NIH – National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research). This is general education and not an endorsement of any product.

Daily habits

Habits That Matter Most for Gum Comfort

1

Brush gently along the gumline with a soft-bristled brush

2

Clean between teeth daily with floss or interdental brushes

3

Avoid tobacco, a major risk factor for gum problems

4

See your dentist if gums bleed, recede or feel sore
