What Is DentaBiome?

DentaBiome is a once-daily oral postbiotic chewable that pairs Lactobacillus postbiotics with xylitol, purple carrot and cranberry. It's designed to support a fresh, balanced mouth.

Introduction

A Supplement Designed for Your Mouth

Most oral care products work on the surface: toothpaste scrubs, mouthwash rinses, floss clears away debris. DentaBiome takes a different angle. It's a daily oral postbiotic, a dietary supplement built around Lactobacillus-derived components, and it comes as a chewable so it does its work in the mouth.

The seller calls it an "advanced oral postbiotic formula for daily oral care." In practice, that means one Berry Frost tablet a day, chewed or slowly dissolved, alongside the brushing and flossing you already do.

Postbiotics are generally described as non-living, stable compounds derived from beneficial bacteria. Unlike live probiotics, they don't rely on keeping organisms alive in the bottle. Wondering where it helps most? Visit the benefits page.

Ingredients

What's Inside Each Chewable

The seller names seven components. Here is what each one is and why it's commonly found in oral care.

Dual-Strain L. plantarum Complex

Lactobacillus plantarum appears as a two-strain complex. The seller presents it as a core part of the oral postbiotic blend, aimed at supporting a balanced community of mouth bacteria.

L. salivarius

A Lactobacillus species named after saliva. It is included in the seller's microbiome-focused blend.

L. rhamnosus

A well-studied Lactobacillus species included alongside the other strains in the blend.

BioFresh™ Clean Complex

A proprietary blend the seller links to DentaBiome's fresh-mouth feel. Its individual components are not disclosed.

Xylitol

A sugar alcohol sweetener that is common in sugar-free gum and oral care products. It gives the chewable its sweetness without adding table sugar.

Purple Carrot Powder

Purple carrots get their deep color from anthocyanin pigments. The powder is listed on the front label as a featured ingredient.

Cranberry Extract

Cranberries contain plant compounds called proanthocyanidins. The extract is the second featured ingredient on the front label.

Ingredient names come from the seller's official page and the front label. The seller does not publish individual amounts or strain counts, so we do not list any.
